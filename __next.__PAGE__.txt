We show that a pretrained video generator can be repurposed into a feed-forward 4D perceiver without iterative denoising. Building on on-demand querying, Gen4P processes clean video latents in a single encoder pass and predicts the requested 4D attributes through a lightweight cross-attention decoder. The on-demand query formulation enables flexible spatial sampling, and our proposed continuous-time queries further provide temporal flexibility. Gen4P achieves state-of-the-art or competitive performance on tasks of 3D point tracking, video depth and camera pose estimation. Qualitative results on in-the-wild videos illustrate its capability of tracking complex motion while maintaining the delicate structure. Together, these results demonstrate the potential of video generative pretraining for unified 4D perception.
