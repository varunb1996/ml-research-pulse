Paper of the Week
9/10 excitement

Large-step adversarial training sharpens robustness, accelerates convergence, and reveals bias.

Yi Feng, Andrea Paudice · cs.LG

Adversarial training traditionally uses tiny perturbation steps, limiting robustness gains and making optimization fragile.

The authors show that deterministic gradient descent with much larger step sizes introduces an implicit bias that steers models toward flatter minima, improving both robustness and convergence speed.

Practitioners can achieve stronger defenses with the same compute budget and gain clearer intuition about the trade‑offs between robustness, sharpness, and generalization.

Key Takeaway

When training robust models, try deterministic GD with step sizes an order of magnitude larger than typical.
