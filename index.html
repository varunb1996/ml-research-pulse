Monocular video now yields full 4D geometry and motion effortlessly

Yitong Xia, Zeren Jiang, Chuanxia Zheng · cs.CV

Problem: Recovering full 4D scene structure from a single moving camera remains compute‑heavy and brittle.

Key insight: Gen4P repurposes a pretrained video generator as a latent 4D perceiver, jointly optimizing geometry and motion without explicit depth supervision.

Why it matters: Practitioners can obtain dense 4D reconstructions with a single forward pass per frame, opening rapid prototyping for AR, robotics, and content creation.

Key Takeaway

Run the released Gen4P inference script on your own video to obtain 4D output without extra training.
