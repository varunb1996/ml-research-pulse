Paper of the Week

Direct Preference Optimization steers diffusion LLMs without RLHF training loop

Bram Wallace, Mei-Hua Dang, Rafael Rafailov · Semantic Scholar

Generating high‑quality text with diffusion models still relies on costly RLHF pipelines. The paper reframes alignment as a direct preference optimization (DPO) problem, sidestepping reinforcement learning altogether.

The authors derive a simple loss that matches the model’s output distribution to human‑ranked comparisons, and they demonstrate stable convergence on several instruction‑tuned diffusion backbones.

Practitioners gain a drop‑in fine‑tuning recipe that runs on a single GPU, cuts training time by >70%, and preserves the diversity inherent to diffusion sampling.

Key Takeaway

Apply DPO to fine‑tune diffusion‑based LLMs directly on preference data, no RL loop needed.
